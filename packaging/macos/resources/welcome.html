AudioRack

An audio effects host shaped like a virtual 19″ equipment rack. This installer places the formats you choose into their standard macOS locations:

  • VST3 Plug-in → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone Application → /Applications

On the next screens you can review the licence and deselect any format you do not need.

After installing, rescan or restart your DAW so it picks up the new plug-in.
